### Celebrity Clue Scene — current preferred rules

- Designed for a large group (~400 members), **one SFW picture posted once**, playable while the host sleeps.
- Members must **guess the actual celebrity, not simply the fictional character**, and ideally explain **at least three** of the clues.
- **Five clues** to one person: **two hard, two medium, one easier**.
- Clues should span **the celebrity's life, background, career, achievements and interests**, not five props from the same famous character.
- No celebrity name, programme or film title, character name, signature costume, or answer on the picture. Avoid literal text labels that solve the puzzle.
- No scheduled follow-up clues required. The host can reveal the answer and explain each clue later.
- Aim for a fair, specific combination that supports **one defensible celebrity**. No guaranteed duration of guessing; large-group members may solve quickly.
- Keep the **answer key separate** from the poster.
- **Status convention:** Previously used / Created or tested / Played (date if known) / Results recorded (only with evidence).

### Other Mystery Monday variants

**Square Reveal:** The Pineapple website covers and progressively uncovers the image; the **underlying artwork must not contain any squares**.

**Progressive Clue Reveal:** The host posts increasingly easier clues; not ideal for unattended overnight play.

**Mystery Member / Mystery Object / Mystery Location / Who Am I?:** Existing Mystery Monday categories. Occasional fictional Murder Mystery is a separate special.
