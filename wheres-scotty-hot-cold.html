WHERE'S SCOTTY?

🔥 HOT OR COLD ❄️

Guess a location. Follow the heat. Find Scotty!

🎮 How to play

Scotty is hiding somewhere in the world. Players suggest a town or city in Telegram. The host checks the distance from the secret location, awards the matching heat rating and adds the guess to the chart. Use everyone's clues to narrow down the hiding place. First correct location wins!
