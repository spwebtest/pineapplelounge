## Hosting
Workdays: self-running first, low-maintenance second. Days off: live specials when desired. Prepare polls in advance. Sunday defaults to relaxed low-effort entertainment. Saturday is the preferred live-special slot, not a requirement.

## Seasonal planning
Keep photo/outfit themes separate from games. Christmas games and dates remain in their existing planning records; this is not a replacement calendar.
